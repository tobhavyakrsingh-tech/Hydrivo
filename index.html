Every wash sends clean-ish water straight down the drain

A washing machine uses a lot of water every cycle, often tens of litres. Once the clothes are done, that water goes into the drain. It is dirty with soap and lint, but it is not sewage, and most of what we use water for at home (mopping, watering, wiping floors) doesn’t need drinking-quality water at all.

The waste

We clean floors and water plants with fresh tap water that was treated to drinking standards, then throw away perfectly usable washer water. Both ends of the loop waste something.

The idea

Catch the washer’s used water (called “greywater”), clean out the lint, hair, soap and smell, keep it in a tank, and reuse it for the jobs that don’t need fresh water. The exact amount you save depends on your machine and how often you wash.

How HYDRIVO works, step by step

Five simple steps. Each one explains what happens in plain words, and what the parts are actually doing.

  1. 1

    Collect: the washer’s used water is caught

    A flexible ribbed hose connects the washing machine’s drain outlet to the HYDRIVO inlet on its side. Instead of going to the wall drain, the water flows into HYDRIVO under gravity and the washer’s own pump.

    In simple words: it’s like moving the washer’s drain pipe into a smart bucket.
  2. 2

    Filter: lint, hair, soap and smell are removed

    The water first passes a lint mesh that catches hair and fibres, then a 5-micron cartridge that removes fine dirt, soap residue and oil. Advanced uses two cartridge stages and tracks how worn the filter is.

    In simple words: like a coffee filter that gets finer in each layer, so the water comes out clear and fresh-smelling. It is for cleaning and plants, not for drinking.
  3. 3

    Store: the clean water waits in a tank

    Filtered water drops into a 50–100 litre storage tank inside the unit. A vertical window on the front, lit in blue, shows how full it is at a glance. When the tank is full, extra water is sent on to the drain so it can never overflow onto your floor.

    In simple words: a water battery. It charges while you wash and you use it when you need it.
  4. 4

    Reuse: a pump gives you the water when you want it

    To keep the price low, Basic has no pump. The tank sits on a raised stand, so gravity brings the water to a tap at the bottom front. Open it to fill a bucket or watering can for mopping, plants or cleaning. Advanced adds an automated pump that pushes water to a side outlet by itself.

    In simple words: a tap for washer water. No lifting, no carrying, no waiting.
  5. 5

    Stay informed: the Advanced model watches over everything

    The smart control unit reads the sensors, the touch display shows tank level and status, and the Wi-Fi module sends it all to your phone app. You get filter-life alerts, real-time usage insights and a running total of the water you’ve saved.

    In simple words: your phone tells you when the filter needs changing and how much water you’ve saved.

Setup and care

Place it

Stand HYDRIVO on a flat floor next to your washing machine. Basic sits on its own raised stand; Advanced needs a power socket nearby.

Connect it

Fit the washer’s drain hose into the inlet (and plug in Advanced). That’s the whole installation. Check the fittings are tight.

Care for it

Watch the filter. Basic: check it by eye and replace it when it looks dirty. Advanced: the app alerts you. Use mild detergents, which are kinder to plants and filters.

Questions people ask

Is the water safe to drink?

No. It is filtered for non-drinking uses only. Never drink it, cook with it or bathe in it.

Will it smell?

Filtering removes most of the soap and odour. Stored water can still turn smelly if it sits for a long time, so use it within a couple of days.

What happens when the tank is full?

The extra water is sent to the drain automatically, so the unit never overflows. Use some water and the tank starts filling again.

Does it need electricity?

Basic needs none: the water flows by gravity. Advanced uses a little mains power for the pump, screen and Wi-Fi.

Will it work with my washing machine?

HYDRIVO connects to the washer’s standard drain hose. It suits most front-load and top-load machines. Check your machine’s hose size before buying.

Which detergent should I use?

Mild, plant-friendly detergents work best. Heavy bleach or strong chemicals make the water unsuitable for plants.
